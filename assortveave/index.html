AssortVeave™

The right product, in the right place — your way.

AssortVeave (pronounced “Assort Weave”) is an AI-assisted assortment intelligence engine for brands that sell through stores they don’t own: distributors, wholesale customers and retail partners. It helps you decide which products each store should carry, explains why, and shows the effect of a change before you commit to it.

Every brand wants the right product in the right store — but “right” isn’t the same for luxury, footwear, apparel, or fragrance, and the more premium and scarce the product, the more it costs to get wrong.

FAQ

Assortment intelligence — common questions.

What is AssortVeave?

AssortVeave is an AI-assisted assortment intelligence engine for brands that sell through stores they don’t own: distributors, wholesale customers and retail partners. It helps you decide which products each store should carry, explains why, and shows the effect of a change before you commit to it. It moulds to your own assortment logic instead of forcing every category into fixed grades.

Is AssortVeave a retail assortment planning tool?

No. Retail assortment tools plan ranges for stores a retailer owns. AssortVeave is for the brand, governing what partner stores carry.

What is assortment intelligence for a brand?

For a brand, assortment intelligence means deciding which products each partner store should carry, and being able to explain why. AssortVeave scores every store and store cluster on the criteria that matter to the brand, derives each store’s assortment from those scores, and shows the effect of a change before you commit to it.

What is selective distribution, and how does AssortVeave support it?

Selective distribution is a model in which a brand sells only through stores it has selected against defined criteria. AssortVeave supports it by scoring each store and store cluster on the brand’s own criteria, such as brand fit and commercial performance, and deriving which products each store can carry. It creates a customer-specific assortment for each customer, with the reason behind every decision.

Does AssortVeave work with SAP?

Yes. AssortVeave creates a customer-specific assortment for each customer, and that assortment goes into SAP and into the B2B ordering portal your customers order from.

Does it replace my merchandising team?

No. AssortVeave gives your team the why and the what-if behind every decision — people still decide and sign off. Governance and a full audit trail are built in.

Which categories does it suit best?

Any brand steering product through tiered or selective retail. It is sharpest where product is premium and scarce — luxury, footwear, apparel, fragrance — where getting the assortment wrong is most costly.
